    About Voice Studio

    Voice Studio was created by Moutter Rasool, a developer from Faisalabad, Pakistan, who builds accessible tools for blind and visually impaired people. It began as a plug-in for the JieShuo screen reader, where students needed to turn very long syllabi into audio.

    What it does

    It splits long text at paragraph and sentence ends, reads every part with Google’s Gemini speech models, switches between your keys when a quota runs out, and joins the result into one file.

    Who it is for

    Students, teachers, video creators, podcasters and freelancers who need clear narration without recording it by hand.

    Accessibility

    The whole site works with a keyboard and a screen reader. If something is hard to use, please tell us.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 10 November 2026

    The short version

    Voice Studio has no accounts and no database. We do not receive your text, your keys or your audio.

    What stays in your browser

    Your API keys and settings are saved in your browser’s local storage so you do not have to enter them again. You can remove them at any time with the Remove buttons or by clearing your browser data. Your text and audio are kept in memory while the page is open and are gone when you close it.

    What is sent to Google

    When you generate audio, your browser sends your text and your key directly to Google’s Gemini API. Google’s own terms apply to that data. On the free quota, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Do not submit private, confidential or sensitive text with a free key. Paid quota is handled under Google’s paid terms.

    Hosting and cookies

    The website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and time of visit. We use no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

    Children
