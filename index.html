    Guides & Frequently Asked Questions

    Practical workflows for students, screen-reader users, educators, and content creators.

    How your daily free allowance works

    Every verified Voice Studio account receives 3,000 free characters every day (roughly 3–4 minutes of natural speech, enough for daily notes, articles, and previews). Your balance resets automatically every day at UTC midnight.

    How to convert long PDF textbooks and syllabi cleanly

    Academic PDFs often contain repeated chapter headers, footers, and page numbers on every page that interrupt listening. Keep Smart document cleanup checked before clicking Load PDF, DOCX, EPUB or TXT. Voice Studio reads the document locally in your browser, strips repeated running headers and standalone page numbers, rejoins hyphenated line breaks, and places only the clean readable text into the editor.

    Why MP3 vs WAV matters for long audio

    Uncompressed WAV audio at 24 kHz uses roughly 170 MB per hour. When listening to a study guide on a mobile phone or sharing lectures on WhatsApp or Google Classroom, choose MP3 (64 kbps)—it reduces file size by roughly 90% while keeping human speech crystal clear. Choose WAV or MP3 (128 kbps) when editing voiceovers.

    Fixing pronunciation for Urdu, Arabic, and technical terms

    Open the Pronunciation dictionary inside step 1 to map abbreviations or terms to phonetic spellings—for example, mapping Dr. to Doctor or custom screen-reader terms. These replacements run automatically right before speech synthesis without changing your saved notes.

    Creating two-speaker dialogues and study podcasts

    Switch Narration mode to Dialogue / 2 speakers, choose distinct voices for Speaker A and Speaker B, and prefix lines with Host: and Guest: (or A: and B:). Voice Studio automatically synthesizes each speaker turn with its assigned Gemini voice and blends them smoothly with your chosen silence gap.

    About Voice Studio

    Voice Studio was created by Moutter Rasool, a developer from Faisalabad, Pakistan, who builds accessible tools for blind and visually impaired people. It began as a plug-in for the JieShuo screen reader, where students needed to turn very long syllabi into audio.

    What it does

    It reads PDF, DOCX, EPUB, and plain text documents directly in your browser, cleans repeated page headers, splits text at paragraph and sentence ends, generates natural speech securely on our server, and joins the result into one MP3 or WAV file.

    Who it is for

    Students, teachers, blind and low-vision readers, video creators, podcasters and freelancers who need clear narration without recording it by hand.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 14 November 2026

    The short version

    Voice Studio stores only your account profile and character usage counts needed to provide your daily allowance. We do not store your documents, uploaded files, or generated audio files on our servers.

    What stays in your browser

    Your voice preferences and pronunciation dictionary rules are saved in your browser’s local storage. Documents you load (PDF, DOCX, EPUB, TXT) are parsed entirely inside your browser, and generated MP3 or WAV audio stays in memory while the page is open.

    Account data & speech processing

    We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to manage sign-in (Google or Email/Password) and daily character counters. When you generate audio, only the text chunk being spoken is sent through our secure Cloudflare Worker backend to Google’s Gemini API, and returned immediately to your browser without being stored.

    Hosting and cookies

    Cloudflare and Firebase may process standard security and session tokens required for authentication and abuse prevention. We use no advertising trackers.
