    Guides & Study Workflows

    Simple steps for turning textbooks, handouts, and scripts into clean MP3 audiobooks and subtitled study videos.

    Quick Summary:

    Open any PDF, EPUB, DOCX, Markdown, or TXT file on your device, keep Smart Document Cleanup enabled to skip headers and page numbers, and download chapter MP3s, WAV audio, or synced subtitles.

    1. Clean Up Academic PDFs Automatically

    Stop page numbers and repeated headers from being read aloud

    University handouts often repeat titles, page numbers, and citation brackets on every page. With Smart document cleanup checked, Voice Studio removes repeating top/bottom lines, standalone page numbers, and broken line-end hyphens automatically.

    2. Fix Urdu & English Pronunciations

    Keep technical terms clear without changing your notes

    Add abbreviations such as Dr., Eq., or technical acronyms to the Pronunciation dictionary in Step 1. Voice Studio adjusts how they are spoken in the audio while keeping your written text untouched.

    3. Split Books into Chapter Audio ZIPs

    Download numbered MP3 tracks and matching captions together

    Load a DRM-free EPUB or textbook and click Split into chapters to pick individual sections, or use Export all chapters as Audiobook ZIP to save numbered MP3 files with synced .srt captions.

    4. Record Specific Pages or Two-Speaker Podcasts

    Extract exact pages or switch between Host and Guest voices

    Use AI PDF & Book Studio to pull out a specific chapter, page range, or scanned PDF page, or open Podcast & Translator for Host: / Guest: dialogues.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Clear answers on cleaning up PDFs, saving mobile data, Urdu and English voices, subtitles, and daily free limits.

    How does Voice Studio prevent page numbers and headers from interrupting study audio?

    When Smart Document Cleanup is enabled, Voice Studio checks your PDF or EPUB on your device, skips repeated header and footer lines, removes standalone page numbers, and rejoins split words before generating audio.

    How big is an hour of speech audio, and does it work well over mobile data or WhatsApp?

    At the default 64 kbps speech MP3 setting, a 60-minute lecture is only about 28 MB—small enough for WhatsApp study groups and low-storage phones. For video editing software, switch to 128 kbps MP3 or WAV in Step 1.

    Does Voice Studio handle mixed Urdu and English study notes accurately?

    Yes. Both Urdu and English sentences are read naturally in one pass. For localized names or technical acronyms, add a replacement rule in the Pronunciation Dictionary so your original text stays untouched.

    How do the 3,000 daily free characters and Unlimited Mode differ?

    Verified accounts receive 3,000 free daily characters with no API key setup required. For entire books or heavy daily use, turn on Own Gemini API Key in Step 1; your key stays strictly inside your browser.

    Are my uploaded documents or generated MP3 files kept on any server?

    No. Your uploaded files, chapter splits, and downloaded MP3, subtitle, and video exports stay on your device without file storage on our servers.

    How do two-speaker dialogues and synchronized .SRT subtitles work?

    Set Narration Mode to Dialogue / 2 speakers, choose Speaker A and Speaker B voices, and prefix lines with Host: / Guest: or A: / B:. Every finished recording automatically builds synced .srt and .vtt caption files.

    How does burned-in subtitle video export work?

    After generating audio, open Export Audio, Subtitles & Video, pick 16:9, 9:16 vertical, or 1:1 square, and press Download video with burned-in subtitles to save a video with clear RTL Urdu or LTR English captions.

    What happens if a PDF is a scanned image without selectable text?

    Voice Studio checks whether a PDF has selectable text and lets you know if pages are scanned images. Open the AI PDF & Book Studio to convert only the scanned pages you need.

    Why Voice Studio Exists

    Long textbooks, multi-chapter EPUB books, and bilingual Urdu-English handouts are difficult to listen to cleanly when standard readers read page numbers, headers, and citations aloud on every single page or produce oversized files that are slow on mobile data.

    Privacy Policy

    Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Operator: Moutter Rasool (Faisalabad, Pakistan)

    1. Document & Media Data (Processed on Your Device)

    Uploaded PDF, EPUB, Word (DOCX), Markdown, and TXT files stay in your browser. Voice Studio does not upload source files for storage or keep finished MP3, WAV, ZIP, .SRT, .VTT, or video downloads on any server.

    2. Browser Preferences (localStorage)

    Preferences—including selected voice, bitrate, speaking style, custom Pronunciation Dictionary rules, display accessibility settings, and any personal Gemini API key you enter for Unlimited Mode—are saved strictly in your browser. Clear site data in your browser settings or select Clear saved key to erase them immediately.

    3. Account & Daily Allowance Records

    To manage your 3,000-character daily free allowance fairly, encrypted authentication and account records store your user ID, email address, verification status, daily character count, credit balance, and activity timestamps. No advertising trackers or third-party ad networks are used.

    4. Temporary Speech Requests

    When using the shared speech allowance, only the short text passage currently being voiced (or the selected script/pages submitted to the PDF or Podcast tools) is sent over encrypted HTTPS to Google’s Gemini speech service and streamed back immediately without archiving your document.

    5. Contact Form Messages

    Submissions sent through the Contact page record your name, reply email, topic, and message inside access-restricted Cloud Firestore storage solely so the operator can respond. Contact records are never sold or shared with marketers.

    6. Access, Export & Account Deletion

    You may request removal of your account profile, quota ledger, or prior contact messages anytime through the Contact page using the topic Privacy / Account Deletion Request.
