    Academic PDFs Without Page-Number Interruptions

    How Smart Document Cleanup repairs multi-page handouts

    University handouts and legal PDFs repeat headers, footers, and page numbers on every page, breaking concentration when listened to linearly. Keeping Smart document cleanup checked in Step 2 scans repeated top/bottom lines across pages locally, removes stray page digits, and rejoins words split by line-end hyphens before synthesis.

    Bilingual Urdu & English Lectures and Notes

    Phonetic accuracy with local Pronunciation Dictionary rules

    Gemini neural voices transition smoothly between English technical terms and Urdu explanations. When an abbreviation or assistive-tech brand gets mispronounced, open Pronunciation dictionary in Step 1 and map written text such as Jieshuo to Jay-shwoh or Dr. to Doctor without altering your original text.

    Mobile Data & WhatsApp Sharing vs. Studio Editing

    Choosing 64 kbps MP3 (~28 MB/hr) or 24 kHz WAV

    Uncompressed 24 kHz PCM WAV requires roughly 170 MB per hour of speech—ideal for video timeline editing in Premiere, CapCut, or DaVinci Resolve. For long textbook chapters revised on a phone or shared across student WhatsApp groups, keep the default MP3 (64 kbps) setting to shrink one hour of spoken audio to about 28 MB.

    Multi-Chapter EPUB Books & Textbook ZIPs

    Chapter splitting + synchronized .SRT & .VTT captions

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Straight answers about privacy, file sizes, bilingual Urdu and English pronunciation, screen-reader compatibility, and daily usage limits.

    How does Voice Studio prevent page numbers and headers from interrupting study audio?

    When Smart Document Cleanup is enabled, Voice Studio parses your PDF or EPUB locally, detects recurring header/footer strings across pages, removes standalone page numbers, and rejoins words split across line-end hyphens before synthesis.

    How big is an hour of speech audio, and does it work well over mobile data or WhatsApp?

    At the default 64 kbps speech MP3 bitrate, a 60-minute lecture fits in roughly 28 MB—small enough for WhatsApp study groups and low-storage phones. For desktop video editing, switch to 128 kbps MP3 or 24 kHz uncompressed WAV in Step 1.

    Does Voice Studio handle mixed Urdu and English study notes accurately?

    Yes. Gemini neural voices read both Urdu and English paragraphs smoothly in one pass. For localized names, technical acronyms, or screen-reader names like Jieshuo, add a replacement in the Pronunciation Dictionary so your source text stays untouched.

    How do the 3,000 daily free characters and Unlimited Mode differ?

    Verified accounts receive 3,000 free daily characters funded by the shared server allowance with no API key setup. For entire books or heavy daily production, turn on Own Gemini API Key in Step 1; your key stays strictly inside browser localStorage.

    Are my uploaded documents or generated MP3 files kept on any server?

    No. Document parsing, chapter splitting, MP3 encoding, and ZIP creation execute directly in browser memory. Only the short text chunk currently being voiced is forwarded transiently to Gemini with zero document storage.

    How do two-speaker dialogues and synchronized .SRT subtitles work?

    Set Narration Mode to Dialogue / 2 speakers, choose Speaker A and Speaker B voices, and prefix turns with Host: / Guest: or A: / B:. Every rendered track also calculates cue timestamps automatically for optional .srt and .vtt subtitle downloads.

    Which screen readers and accessibility practices are verified?

    Voice Studio is authored by a blind software developer with WCAG-aligned headings, skip links, 44px touch targets, inert navigation-drawer focus trapping, and polite/assertive live regions tested across Jieshuo, Android TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver.

    What happens if a PDF is a scanned photo without selectable text?

    Voice Studio checks for an embedded text layer locally and notifies you immediately if pages are image-only scans, so you never waste character quota on empty output.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 16 November 2026

    Summary

    Voice Studio stores only the account metadata and daily character counters required to operate your free allowance. Uploaded PDF, DOCX, EPUB, and TXT documents are never persisted on Voice Studio servers, and rendered MP3, WAV, or ZIP files remain strictly on your device.

    Data kept exclusively in your browser

    Voice presets, export bitrate choices, custom Pronunciation Dictionary entries, and optional personal Gemini API keys (if Unlimited Mode is turned on) live strictly inside your browser’s localStorage. File extraction and audio assembly occur entirely in local memory.

    Account authentication & transient speech requests

    Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore verify accounts and track daily character allowances. On the shared server tier, only the active speech chunk is sent transiently to Google’s Gemini API and streamed right back without disk storage.

    Contact form submissions

    Inquiries sent through the Contact page store your name, email address, topic, and message inside access-restricted Firestore storage strictly so the developer can answer you. Contact records are never sold or shared with third-party marketers.

    Infrastructure & essential storage

    Hosting layers (Vercel, Cloudflare Workers, and Firebase) handle routine security and rate-protection requests. Local browser storage is used strictly for signed-in session state and workspace settings.

    Eligibility & deletion requests

    Voice Studio is intended for users aged 18 or older, or students using the service with guardian or educational supervision. Request account deletion or data clarification anytime via our Contact page.
