    Guides & Workflows

    Built as a screen reader TTS studio for blind students, educators, and creators using NVDA, JAWS, TalkBack, VoiceOver, and Jieshuo.

    Quick Answer:

    Voice Studio converts PDF, EPUB, Word DOCX, and Urdu/English study notes directly inside your browser into one clean MP3 or WAV file—giving every verified user 3,000 free characters daily plus an optional browser-only Unlimited Mode.

    Free PDF to Audio Converter for Blind & Low-Vision Readers

    Automatic header, footer, and page-number cleanup

    Academic PDFs and university syllabi often repeat chapter headers, footers, and page numbers on every page, interrupting listening. Keep Smart document cleanup checked before selecting Load PDF, DOCX, EPUB or TXT. Voice Studio reads the file privately inside your browser, removes repeated page clutter, rejoins hyphenated line breaks, and loads clean text ready for speech.

    Natural Urdu & English Text to Speech

    Pronunciation dictionary for local names and screen readers

    Gemini voices naturally pronounce both Urdu and English paragraphs, including bilingual study notes. Open the Pronunciation dictionary in Step 1 to map abbreviations, assistive tech names like Jieshuo (pronounced phonetically as Jay-shwoh), or local terms to exact spoken spellings without altering your original notes.

    EPUB Audiobook Maker & DOCX Study Notes

    Choosing MP3 (64 kbps) vs. WAV audio

    Load full DRM-free EPUB ebooks or Word DOCX handouts to assemble a single continuous audio file. Uncompressed WAV at 24 kHz uses around 170 MB per hour, whereas MP3 (64 kbps) shrinks file size by roughly 90% so lectures play smoothly on mobile phones and share easily over WhatsApp or Classroom.

    Daily 3,000 Free Characters & Unlimited Mode

    Convert full textbooks at zero cost

    Every verified account receives 3,000 free characters daily with zero API key setup. For full textbooks or multi-hour audiobooks, open Own Gemini API Key (optional Unlimited Mode) in Step 1 and paste your free Google AI Studio key. Your key stays strictly in your browser’s localStorage and bypasses the 3,000-character daily cap.

    Two-Speaker Dialogues & Study Podcasts

    Host and Guest narration in one file

    Switch Narration mode to Dialogue / 2 speakers, choose distinct voices for Speaker A and Speaker B, and prefix lines with Host: / Guest: or A: / B:. Voice Studio synthesizes each speaker turn cleanly with your chosen pause gap.

    YouTube, Reels & E-Learning Voiceovers

    Consistent pacing and custom style instructions

    Add an optional style direction such as calmly, clearly, at a steady documentary pace, preview the first 200 characters before rendering the full script, and export 128 kbps MP3 or uncompressed WAV directly into your video editor.

    Supported Formats & Audio Export

    Voice Studio extracts text locally in your browser from standard document formats and joins generated speech into MP3 or uncompressed WAV audio.

    Summary:

    Import PDF, EPUB, DOCX, Markdown, or TXT locally without uploading documents to a storage server, then export continuous 64 kbps MP3 (~28 MB/hour), 128 kbps MP3, or 24 kHz WAV.

    Format / Feature How It Works Best For
    PDF (.pdf) Client-side text layer extraction with optional header, footer, and page-number cleanup Research papers, exam syllabi, textbooks, legal briefings
    EPUB (.epub) Multi-chapter DRM-free ebook text extraction directly in browser Turning full ebooks into portable MP3 audiobooks
    Word (.docx) Clean paragraph extraction from handouts, essays, and scripts Lecture notes, assignments, course voiceovers
    TXT & Markdown (.txt, .md) Direct paste, clipboard import, or drag-and-drop conversion YouTube narration, Reels scripts, study flashcards
    MP3 (64 kbps / 128 kbps) Smooth cross-faded speech encoding (~28 MB per hour at 64 kbps) Mobile listening, WhatsApp sharing, low-storage phones
    WAV (24 kHz PCM) Uncompressed lossless waveform output with zero encoding delay Video editors, podcast mastering, archival narration

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about accessible PDF, EPUB, and Word DOCX conversion, Urdu & English voices, privacy, and daily free allowances.

    How does the free PDF to audio converter for blind users work?

    Voice Studio reads text layers from PDF, DOCX, EPUB, and TXT files directly in your browser, removes repeated running headers and page numbers, splits cleanly at sentence boundaries, and announces progress politely to screen readers like Jieshuo, NVDA, JAWS, TalkBack, and VoiceOver.

    Does Voice Studio support Urdu and English text to speech?

    Yes. Selected Gemini speech models pronounce both English and Urdu naturally—including mixed bilingual academic notes. You can also add custom phonetic rules in the Pronunciation Dictionary so names, Arabic or Urdu terms, and technical acronyms sound right.

    Can I convert an EPUB ebook or Word DOCX file into one MP3 audiobook?

    Yes. Drop any DRM-free EPUB ebook or Word DOCX file into Step 2, keep Smart Document Cleanup enabled, and Voice Studio joins all generated chunks seamlessly into one MP3 or WAV audio file.

    How do the 3,000 daily free characters and Unlimited Mode work?

    Every verified account receives 3,000 free characters every day with zero API key setup. For full textbooks, expand Own Gemini API Key (optional Unlimited Mode) in Step 1 and save your own Google AI Studio key strictly in browser localStorage.

    Are my documents or generated MP3 files saved on Voice Studio servers?

    No. Document parsing and audio joining happen inside your browser. Neither your uploaded PDF/DOCX/EPUB files nor your downloaded audio files are stored on Voice Studio servers.

    How do I make a two-speaker AI podcast or dialogue recording?

    Switch Narration Mode to Dialogue / 2 speakers, select distinct voices for Speaker A and Speaker B, and prefix lines with Host: / Guest: or A: / B:. Each line switches voices automatically into one single audio file.

    Which screen readers is Voice Studio tested and designed for?

    Voice Studio follows WCAG 2.1 AAA principles with skip links, logical headings, 44px touch targets, inert modal navigation drawer isolation, and live status regions tailored for Jieshuo, TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver.

    What if my PDF is a scanned image without selectable text?

    If a PDF contains scanned page photos with no embedded text layer, Voice Studio informs you immediately so you can run OCR before converting the extracted text to speech.

    About Voice Studio

    Voice Studio was created by Moutter Rasool (AlphaAutomates), a blind 11th-grade software developer from Faisalabad, Pakistan, who builds practical assistive tools for blind and visually impaired students, educators, and creators.

    Why We Built It (First-Hand Experience)

    Voice Studio began as a workflow plug-in for the Jieshuo (Commentary) Android screen reader, where blind students needed a reliable way to turn long academic PDFs, Urdu and English syllabi, and DRM-free EPUB textbooks into portable audio they could revise offline without tiring their screen reader or losing sentence flow.

    How It Works

    Documents loaded into Voice Studio (PDF, Word DOCX, EPUB, Markdown, or TXT) are extracted entirely inside your web browser. Smart document cleanup strips repeated headers, footers, and page numbers, applies your custom Pronunciation Dictionary rules, and joins speech synthesis output cleanly into one downloadable MP3 or uncompressed WAV track.

    Accessibility & Quality Standards

    • Screen-Reader First: Designed and tested with Jieshuo, Android TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver using WCAG 2.1 AAA semantics, skip links, 44px touch targets, and polite/assertive live status announcements.
    • Zero File Retention: Your documents and finished MP3/WAV exports are never stored on Voice Studio servers.
    • Transparent Free Tier & Unlimited Mode: Verified accounts start immediately with 3,000 free characters daily, plus an optional browser-only personal Gemini API key mode for full textbooks.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 15 November 2026

    The short version

    Voice Studio stores only the account metadata and daily character counters needed to run your free allowance. We never store your uploaded PDF, DOCX, EPUB, or TXT files, and we never save your finished MP3 or WAV audio files on our servers.

    What stays strictly inside your browser

    Your voice settings, output format choices, Pronunciation Dictionary rules, and optional personal Gemini API key (if you activate Unlimited Mode) are stored strictly in your browser’s localStorage. Documents you select are extracted locally inside your browser and audio merging runs in browser memory.

    Account data & speech synthesis

    Voice Studio uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to handle verified sign-in and daily free character balances. When you generate speech via the shared server tier, only the active text chunk being synthesized is forwarded to Google’s Gemini API and streamed back immediately without retention.

    Direct contact messages

    When you send a message on the Contact page, only your provided name, email address, topic, and message are saved securely in restricted Firestore storage so the developer can review and respond to your inquiry. We never sell, rent, or publish visitor contact details.

    Hosting, security & cookies

    Hosting infrastructure (Vercel, Cloudflare Workers, and Firebase) processes standard session and anti-abuse security requests. Essential browser storage is used strictly for authentication state and user preferences. Any future advertising integrations, if introduced, would adhere to Google publisher disclosure and consent policies.

    Eligibility

    Voice Studio is intended for users aged 18 or older, or students using the service with appropriate educational or guardian supervision.

    Account deletion & privacy requests

    To request account removal, data clarification, or report a privacy concern, submit a request anytime through our Contact page.
